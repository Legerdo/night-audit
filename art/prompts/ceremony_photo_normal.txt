A faded color photograph from November 1994 of a building completion ceremony in Seoul, 35mm film print look with soft grain and slightly washed-out colors.
In front of the glass entrance of a newly built 12-storey beige-tiled residential and commercial building, seven Korean adults stand in a single row behind a long red and white ceremonial ribbon stretched across the frame at waist height: men in dark 1990s suits and ties, one woman in a navy skirt suit, and two men in tan work jackets wearing white hard hats. Each person wears white gloves and holds a pair of scissors at the ribbon.
All seven people are looking DOWN at the ribbon in front of them, heads tilted down, mid-ceremony, just about to cut.
Two tall congratulatory flower wreath stands on the left and right edges (the ribbons on them are blank, no writing).
Overcast daylight, eye-level straight-on view from about eight meters away, the entrance and the lower three floors of the building visible behind the people.
