Architectural marketing rendering for a redevelopment apartment advertisement in Korea.
A new high-rise apartment complex at blue hour dusk: three sleek 35-storey residential towers with pale stone and glass facades and warm lit windows, a landscaped park with trees and walking paths in the foreground, a hazy Korean city skyline with mountains behind.
The central tower is the closest and tallest; its flat roof edge is cleanly visible against the sky.
Clean, glossy advertisement style, calm and aspirational, portrait orientation 3:4, generous empty gradient sky in the upper third (reserved for text that will be added later).
Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark.
